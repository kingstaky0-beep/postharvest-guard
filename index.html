Moisture limits vary by commodity, buyer specification and storage conditions. Rather than guessing, enter the target required for the batch.

Simple drying workflow

  1. Spread grain evenly and protect it from unexpected rain or contamination.
  2. Mix/turn as appropriate for your drying method.
  3. Measure representative samples with a suitable meter.
  4. Record each reading and stop when your target specification is reached.
  5. Cool and store in clean, dry, protected conditions.
Important: The app does not certify grain quality or guarantee that a moisture value is safe for every storage or sale situation. Follow your buyer, commodity and local food-safety requirements.
